\summarypart{NINE}{The Age of Global Expansion and Relocation}{1900--}
\begin{partoverview}
Never have so many people turned to Christianity as in the past hundred years, and by that measure
this may be the most eventful century the church has known. Most of the increase lies south of the
Equator. The missionary movement of the decades around 1900 prepared it, yet the newer churches have
grown under their own leaders and, as the book sees it, by the Spirit's own initiative. Meanwhile the
lands that once sent the missionaries, Europe and North America, stand still or shrink.

The book leaves two matters open. Will the churches of the Global South hold to the historic faith as
they grow? And will the West see a new awakening? If it does not, the authors expect the description
``post-Christian'' to become steadily more accurate.
\end{partoverview}
