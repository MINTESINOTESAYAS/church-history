\summarypart{ONE}{The Age of Jesus and the Apostles}{6 BC--AD 70}
\begin{partoverview}
The story opens inside Judaism. Jesus of Nazareth, a Jewish teacher whose message unsettled the
religious leadership of his own people, gathered a movement in Galilee and Judea and was executed by
order of the Roman governor Pontius Pilate. Within a generation his followers had carried their message
through the cities of the eastern Mediterranean. The decisive step was taken by the apostle Paul, who
insisted that God's grace is offered to Gentiles on the same terms as to Jews. With that conviction a
movement born in Palestine became a faith for every people.
\end{partoverview}
