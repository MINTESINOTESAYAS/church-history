\summarypart{ONE}{The Age of Jesus and the Apostles}{6 BC--AD 70}
\begin{partoverview}
Christianity's roots lie deep in Jewish history, long before the birth of Jesus. But it was Jesus of
Nazareth who challenged the established Judaism of his day and, early in the first century, brought a
renewal movement into the light of history. After his crucifixion under the Roman official Pontius
Pilate, his teaching spread around the Mediterranean. The apostle Paul was especially influential: by
stressing that salvation is God's gift for all people, he led Christianity out of Palestinian Judaism
and made it a universal religion.
\end{partoverview}
