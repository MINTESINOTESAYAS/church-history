\noindent\sblabel{About this summary}\par\vspace{6pt}
\noindent This volume is an independent study summary of \booktitle{Church History in Plain Language} by
Bruce L. Shelley, in the fourth edition revised by R.~L.~Hatchett. It keeps the plan of the original---nine ages and forty-eight chapters---and goes through every chapter section by section, under the
original section headings, so that no topic of the book is passed over. The sidebars of the original are
summarized in tinted boxes, and each chapter closes with its key people, dates and terms. The foreword,
the prologue, the nine part introductions and the epilogue are summarized as well; the list of popes is
given in full as an appendix, and a chronology gathers the dates of all chapters in one place.

\medskip
\noindent The summary is written in new words and reports what the book says; where the author takes a
position, the summary says so. Not reproduced are the reading lists, the source notes, the pictures and
the timelines of the original. A summary is a map, not the journey: for the stories told in full, for
the quotations and for the argument, read the book itself.
