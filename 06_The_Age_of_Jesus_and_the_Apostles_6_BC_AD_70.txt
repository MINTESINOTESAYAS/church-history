THE AGE OF JESUS AND
THE APOSTLES
6 BC–AD 70
Christianity’s roots go back into Jewish history long before the birth of
Jesus Christ. It was Jesus of Nazareth, however, who attacked established
Judaism and brought a renewal movement into history’s light early in the
first century. After his crucifixion under Pontius Pilate, a Roman official,
Jesus’ teachings spread throughout the Mediterranean area. An apostle
named Paul was especially influential. He stressed God’s gift of salvation
for all men and thus led in Christianity’s emergence from Palestinian
Judaism to a position as a universal religion.
